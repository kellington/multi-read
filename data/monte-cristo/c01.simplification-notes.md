# Chapter 1 vocabulary and Plain-mode review

This is the first vocabulary-aware baseline, built before a fresh reading pass.
The difficulty map identifies candidates; it does not automatically rewrite
every candidate. The reader keeps original French available in Guided and
Original modes.

## Decisions applied in Plain

- **Preserve and teach:** recurring words such as *armateur*, *barque*,
  *matelot*, and *chargement* stay in French with contextual help.
- **Lightly gloss:** people, ships, and places such as Dantès, the Pharaon,
  Smyrne, and Marseille are explained without treating their names as learner
  vocabulary.
- **Ease selected low-value difficulty:** the lookout becomes *le guetteur*;
  specialized sails and rigging receive more familiar French descriptions;
  *fièvre cérébrale*, *mouillage*, *escompter*, and *obséquieux* have targeted
  first-pass substitutions. Some long clauses are separated when grammar
  permits it.

## What to watch during the restart

- Does Plain preserve the event and tone of each passage while easing actual
  friction? Many passages still retain Dumas's literary syntax.
- Are the words marked **Learn** genuinely useful later in the available
  volume? Are any names or nautical terms taking too much attention?
- Are contextual meanings accurate in their passage, especially for words
  with more than one sense?
- Which hard sentences need sentence meaning, a fuller rewrite, or simply
  original comparison?
